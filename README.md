# BioTrack
**A Smart IoT Vital Sensing and AI Health Advisory Platform with Blockchain-Verified Patient Records**

## Overview

**BioTrack** is a cross-platform digital health platform that integrates **smartphone-based IoT vital sensing**, **AI-powered health advisory**, and **blockchain-secured medical records**. The system enables accessible and preventive health monitoring by utilizing sensors available in modern smartphones, eliminating the need for specialized medical hardware.

The platform supports **United Nations Sustainable Development Goal (SDG) 3: Good Health and Well-Being** by expanding access to essential health monitoring and digital health advisory services, particularly in communities with limited healthcare resources.

## System Components

### Mobile IoT Vital Sensing
BioTrack leverages built-in smartphone sensors to capture physiological and activity-related health indicators:

- **Camera and Flashlight:** Photoplethysmography (PPG) for heart rate measurement  
- **Accelerometer and Gyroscope:** Activity monitoring and fall detection  
- **Signal preprocessing and filtering** to improve measurement reliability

### AI Health Advisory
An **LLM-based advisory module** analyzes collected health indicators and user-reported symptoms to generate:

- Personalized health insights  
- Symptom interpretation  
- Preventive health guidance  

The advisory system is intended to **support health awareness and early risk detection**, and does not replace professional medical diagnosis.

### Blockchain-Secured Medical Records
BioTrack implements **blockchain-based verification** to ensure the integrity and security of patient health records through:

- **Cryptographic hashing** for data integrity  
- **Immutable record verification**  
- **Tamper-resistant storage mechanisms**  
- **Privacy-preserving data management**

### Cross-Platform Infrastructure
The platform consists of:

- **Mobile application:** Android and iOS health monitoring interface  
- **Web dashboard:** Health data visualization and patient record access  
- **Cloud backend:** Data processing, analytics, and secure storage
