# BioTrack  
### Smart IoT Vital Sensing and AI Health Advisory Platform with Blockchain-Verified Patient Records

**BioTrack** is a **cross-platform digital health platform** integrating **smartphone-based IoT vital sensing**, **AI-powered health advisory**, and **blockchain-secured patient records**. It enables accessible, preventive health monitoring without specialized medical hardware and ensures tamper-resistant medical record management.

---

## System Overview

BioTrack facilitates **continuous health monitoring** for users and supports **SDG 3: Good Health and Well-Being** by expanding access to essential health services and digital health advisory tools. Key capabilities include:

- **Real-time vital sign measurement** via smartphone sensors  
- **Personalized health advisory** using AI analysis  
- **Immutable, privacy-preserving medical record storage** on blockchain  
- **Cross-platform accessibility** via mobile and web interfaces  

---

## Core Components

### 1. Mobile IoT Vital Sensing
- Utilizes built-in smartphone sensors for physiological and activity data:  
  - **Camera & Flashlight:** Photoplethysmography (PPG) for heart rate monitoring  
  - **Accelerometer & Gyroscope:** Activity tracking and fall detection  
- **Signal preprocessing and filtering** ensures data reliability and accuracy  

### 2. AI Health Advisory
- **LLM-based advisory module** provides:  
  - Personalized health insights  
  - Symptom interpretation  
  - Preventive guidance  
- Supports **early risk detection** but **does not replace professional medical diagnosis**  

### 3. Blockchain-Secured Medical Records
- Ensures **data integrity, transparency, and tamper-resistance** through:  
  - **Cryptographic hashing** of records  
  - **Immutable verification** on blockchain  
  - **Privacy-preserving storage mechanisms**  

### 4. Cross-Platform Infrastructure
- **Mobile application:** Android and iOS interface for health monitoring  
- **Web dashboard:** Data visualization and patient record access  
- **Cloud backend:** Data processing, analytics, and secure storage
